What is AAROHA?

AROGYA = Health, well-being, or freedom from illness
+
ASHA = Hope

Rural healthcare emergencies occur when people need urgent medical help but face severe delays. AAROHA aims to bridge the rural healthcare gap by bringing timely medical support closer to those in need.

The Current Problems

Ambulance Delays

Due to poor road infrastructure and long distances, emergency vehicles often arrive too late.

Confusion

Lack of a single unified emergency network leads to critical time wasted trying to find the right contact.

Long Distances

Distant Hospitals

Major hospitals are situated far from rural centers, making timely intervention nearly impossible.

Lack of Awareness

(Tap to reveal)

First-Aid Knowledge

Locals often lack basic first-aid training, turning to unprofessional help in critical moments.

Bridging the Gap: Our Proposed Solution

First-Aid Training & Health Camps

Empowering the community with vital skills and awareness to handle immediate emergencies before help arrives.

Local Emergency Volunteers

Replacing dependence on unequipped neighbors with trained, dedicated local response units.

Telemedicine & Consultations

Connecting remote patients instantly with professional doctors through digital channels.
