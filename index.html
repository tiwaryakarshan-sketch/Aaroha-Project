Rural healthcare emergencies occur when people need urgent medical help but face severe delays due to poor roads, long distances, limited facilities, and slow ambulance services.

Barriers to Timely Medical Care

Tap or hover on the cards to explore the issues

Delayed Ambulance

Poor roads and long distances cause fatal delays.

Multiple Numbers

Confusion during panics over which emergency number to call.

Lack of Awareness

Limited knowledge of basic first-aid techniques.

Long Distances

Hospitals are located far away from rural centers.

Limited Support

Lack of advanced medical equipment and professionals.

The AAROHA Impact

BEFORE

  • ❌ Delayed ambulance response
  • ❌ Long distance to hospitals
  • ❌ Limited medical support
  • ❌ Lack of emergency awareness
  • ❌ Dependence on family/neighbours
  • ❌ Unprofessional medical advice

AFTER

  • ✅ Faster emergency response via volunteers
  • ✅ Better doctor access through telemedicine
  • ✅ First-aid trained community members
  • ✅ Health awareness & training
  • ✅ Stronger community support system
  • ✅ Earlier attention and safer outcomes

Conclusion

AAROHA aims to bridge the rural healthcare gap by bringing timely medical support closer to communities. Through emergency response, first-aid training, telemedicine, health awareness, and local volunteers, it can help rural people receive the right care at the right time.
